# Policies

This directory contains sample information security policy templates developed for **Aether Labs**, a fictional small remote-first SaaS company.

These are educational templates intended as a starting point for understanding ISO 27001 policy structure. They are not intended for direct use in a real organization without customization and legal review.

## Available Policies

1. [Information Security Policy](information-security-policy.md) — High-level framework and management commitment.
2. [Acceptable Use of Information and Other Associated Assets](acceptable-use.md) — Rules for responsible use of assets.
3. [Access Control Policy](access-control.md) — Requirements for managing access to systems and data.
4. [Information Security Incident Management](incident-management.md) — Process for handling security incidents.
5. [Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery](business-continuity.md) — Planning for continuity and recovery from disruptions.
6. [Supplier and Third-Party Security](supplier-security.md) — Managing security risks from external parties.
7. [Data Protection and Privacy](data-protection.md) — Requirements for protecting personal data.

## Annex A Cross-References

Each policy includes explicit references to relevant controls from ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A. Examples include:

- A.5.1 Policies for information security
- A.5.10 Acceptable use of information and other associated assets
- A.5.15 Access control
- A.5.19–A.5.22 Supplier security controls
- A.5.24–A.5.30 Incident and continuity controls
- A.5.34 Privacy and protection of PII
- A.6.3–A.6.8 People controls
- A.8.1–A.8.34 Technological controls

See individual policies for full mappings.

## Usage Notes

- Replace "Aether Labs" and fictional details with your organization's information.
- Align with your actual risk assessment and Statement of Applicability.
- Have policies reviewed and approved by appropriate management and legal counsel.
- Review and update policies at least annually.

These templates were created as part of the ISMS ISO Toolkit hobby project to demonstrate practical policy writing aligned with the standard.
