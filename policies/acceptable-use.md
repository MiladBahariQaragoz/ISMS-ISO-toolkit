# Acceptable Use of Information and Other Associated Assets

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Owner:** Information Security Team  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership, Aether Labs  
**Review Date:** 2027-07-11  
**Classification:** Internal

## Purpose
This policy defines the acceptable use of Aether Labs information assets, systems, and resources. It aims to protect the organization from risks associated with misuse while enabling employees to perform their duties effectively (A.5.10).

## Scope
This policy applies to all Aether Labs personnel (employees, contractors, interns) and authorized third parties who use or access Aether Labs information assets, including but not limited to:
- Computers, mobile devices, and networks
- Software, applications, and cloud services
- Data, email, and communication tools
- Physical assets (laptops, storage media)

## Definitions
- **Acceptable Use:** Use that supports business objectives without compromising security, legality, or ethics.
- **Misuse:** Any use that violates this policy, laws, or creates unnecessary risk.

## Policy Statements
1. **Business Use Primary**  
   Information assets shall primarily be used for legitimate business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources, or violate other policies (A.5.10).

2. **No Illegal or Harmful Activity**  
   Users must not use Aether Labs assets to engage in illegal activities, harassment, discrimination, or distribution of offensive material.

3. **Data Protection**  
   Users must handle information according to its classification. Confidential or personal data must not be copied, shared, or stored on unauthorized systems or personal devices without approval (A.5.12, A.5.14).

4. **Authentication and Access**  
   Users are responsible for protecting their credentials. Passwords must not be shared. Sessions must be locked when unattended. Access must be limited to authorized resources (A.5.17).

5. **Software and Hardware**  
   Only approved software may be installed. Users must not modify hardware configurations or bypass security controls. Personal devices used for work must comply with the Remote Working Policy (A.8.1, A.8.19).

6. **Monitoring and Privacy**  
   Users have no expectation of privacy on Aether Labs systems. All activity may be monitored and logged for security, compliance, and operational purposes (A.5.15).

7. **Reporting**  
   Suspected policy violations, security incidents, or misuse must be reported immediately via the incident reporting process (A.6.8).

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Users:** Comply with this policy and use assets responsibly.
- **Managers:** Ensure team members understand and follow the policy.
- **IT/Security Team:** Enforce controls, monitor usage, and investigate violations.
- **Human Resources:** Handle disciplinary actions for breaches.

## Compliance
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination of access or employment, and potential legal action. See Disciplinary Process (A.6.4).

Exceptions require documented approval from the CISO.
