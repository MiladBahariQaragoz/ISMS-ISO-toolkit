# Access Control Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Owner:** Information Security Team  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership, Aether Labs  
**Review Date:** 2027-07-11  
**Classification:** Internal

## Purpose
This policy establishes requirements for controlling access to Aether Labs information assets and systems. The goal is to ensure that only authorized users access information based on business need, following the principle of least privilege (A.5.15, A.5.18).

## Scope
This policy applies to all information systems, applications, data repositories, and physical access controls used by Aether Labs, including cloud services, internal tools, and development environments.

## Definitions
- **Least Privilege:** Granting only the minimum access rights necessary to perform a job function.
- **Access Rights:** Permissions assigned to users, roles, or groups for specific resources.

## Policy Statements
1. **Registration and Deregistration**  
   User accounts must be formally registered before access is granted. Accounts must be promptly disabled or removed upon termination or role change (A.5.16, A.5.18).

2. **Access Provisioning**  
   Access requests must be approved by the resource owner or manager. Provisioning shall follow documented procedures and be limited to approved roles (A.5.15).

3. **Authentication**  
   All users must authenticate using strong methods (e.g., multi-factor authentication where applicable). Passwords and credentials must meet defined complexity and rotation requirements (A.5.17, A.8.5).

4. **Privileged Access**  
   Privileged accounts (admin, root) must be minimized, monitored, and used only when necessary. Separate accounts shall be used for privileged and normal activities (A.8.2).

5. **Review and Revocation**  
   Access rights shall be reviewed at least quarterly. Access must be revoked immediately when no longer required (A.5.18).

6. **Segregation of Duties**  
   Critical functions must be separated so that no single individual can complete a sensitive process alone (A.5.3).

7. **Remote and Mobile Access**  
   Remote access must use approved secure channels (e.g., VPN or equivalent) and comply with the Remote Working Policy (A.6.7).

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Resource Owners:** Authorize and periodically review access to their assets.
- **IT Team:** Implement and enforce technical access controls.
- **Managers:** Approve access for their team members and notify of changes.
- **Users:** Protect their credentials and use only authorized access.

## Compliance
Violations may result in disciplinary action (A.6.4). Unauthorized access attempts will be logged and investigated.
