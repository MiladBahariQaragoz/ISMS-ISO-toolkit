# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Owner:** Operations / Platform Team  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership, Aether Labs  
**Review Date:** 2027-07-11  
**Classification:** Internal

## Purpose
This policy ensures that Aether Labs can continue critical business operations and recover from disruptive events with minimal impact on customers and stakeholders. It defines requirements for business continuity planning and disaster recovery (A.5.29, A.5.30).

## Scope
This policy applies to all business processes, information systems, and facilities supporting the Aether SaaS platform and internal operations.

## Definitions
- **Business Continuity (BC):** Capability to continue operations at an acceptable level after a disruption.
- **Disaster Recovery (DR):** Process of restoring IT systems and data after a major incident.

## Policy Statements
1. **Business Impact Analysis (BIA)**  
   A BIA shall be conducted and updated annually to identify critical processes, dependencies, and recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) (A.5.30).

2. **Continuity Plans**  
   Documented business continuity and disaster recovery plans shall be maintained. Plans must include roles, communication procedures, and alternate processing arrangements (A.5.29).

3. **IT Resilience**  
   Critical systems shall have redundancy and failover capabilities. Backups shall be performed regularly, tested, and stored securely offsite or in the cloud (A.8.13, A.8.14).

4. **Testing and Exercises**  
   BC/DR plans shall be tested at least annually through tabletop exercises, simulations, or full tests. Results shall be documented and plans updated (A.5.30).

5. **Crisis Management**  
   A crisis management team shall be activated for major disruptions. Clear escalation and communication protocols shall be followed.

6. **Third-Party Dependencies**  
   Continuity requirements shall be included in contracts with critical suppliers. Supplier BC capabilities shall be assessed (A.5.22).

7. **Return to Normal Operations**  
   Procedures for safely returning to normal operations after an incident shall be defined and tested.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Executive Leadership:** Approve plans and provide resources.
- **BC/DR Coordinator:** Maintain plans, coordinate testing, and report status.
- **IT/Platform Team:** Implement technical recovery solutions and backups.
- **All Teams:** Participate in planning and exercises relevant to their functions.

## Compliance
Failure to maintain or follow plans may be treated as a policy violation (A.6.4).
