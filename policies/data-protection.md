# Data Protection and Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Owner:** Data Protection Officer (fictional)  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership, Aether Labs  
**Review Date:** 2027-07-11  
**Classification:** Internal

## Purpose
This policy establishes requirements for the protection of personal data and privacy at Aether Labs in accordance with applicable data protection laws (e.g., GDPR) and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (A.5.34).

## Scope
This policy applies to all processing of personal data by or on behalf of Aether Labs, including customer data, employee data, and data processed by third parties.

## Definitions
- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- **Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data (collection, storage, use, disclosure, deletion).

## Policy Statements
1. **Lawfulness and Transparency**  
   Personal data shall be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently. Individuals shall be informed about processing activities through privacy notices (A.5.34).

2. **Purpose Limitation and Data Minimization**  
   Data shall be collected only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes and shall be adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary (A.5.12).

3. **Accuracy and Storage Limitation**  
   Personal data shall be accurate and kept up to date. Data shall not be kept longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected (A.5.33).

4. **Security of Processing**  
   Appropriate technical and organizational measures shall be implemented to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or damage (A.5.15, A.8.12).

5. **Rights of Data Subjects**  
   Individuals have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Requests shall be handled within legal timeframes.

6. **Data Transfers**  
   Personal data shall not be transferred outside approved jurisdictions without adequate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

7. **Breach Notification**  
   Personal data breaches shall be notified to supervisory authorities and affected individuals without undue delay where required by law (A.5.26).

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Data Protection Officer (fictional):** Oversee compliance, advise on data protection, act as contact point.
- **All Teams:** Ensure data processing activities comply with this policy.
- **Third Parties:** Contractually bound to protect personal data.

## Compliance
Non-compliance may result in disciplinary action and regulatory penalties. Data protection impact assessments (DPIAs) shall be conducted for high-risk processing.
