# Information Security Incident Management Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Owner:** Information Security Team  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership, Aether Labs  
**Review Date:** 2027-07-11  
**Classification:** Internal

## Purpose
This policy defines the requirements for managing information security incidents at Aether Labs. The objective is to ensure timely detection, response, recovery, and learning from incidents to minimize impact and prevent recurrence (A.5.24, A.5.26).

## Scope
This policy covers all information security events and incidents affecting Aether Labs assets, services, data, or personnel, including those involving third parties.

## Definitions
- **Event:** An observed occurrence that may indicate a security incident.
- **Incident:** A confirmed security event that has caused or could cause harm.

## Policy Statements
1. **Incident Response Process**  
   All suspected incidents must be reported immediately through the defined channels. A structured response process shall be followed (A.5.24, A.5.25).

2. **Roles and Responsibilities**  
   An Incident Response Team (IRT) shall be designated. Roles include Incident Manager, Technical Responders, and Communications Lead (A.5.26).

3. **Detection and Reporting**  
   Systems shall be monitored for anomalies. Personnel must report events promptly without fear of reprisal (A.6.8).

4. **Containment, Eradication, and Recovery**  
   Incidents shall be contained to limit damage. Root cause shall be identified and eradicated before recovery. Business continuity plans shall be invoked as needed (A.5.29).

5. **Evidence Handling**  
   Evidence shall be collected, preserved, and handled in a forensically sound manner for potential legal or regulatory use (A.5.28).

6. **Communication**  
   Internal and external communications shall be controlled. Customers and regulators shall be notified as required by law or contract (A.5.5).

7. **Post-Incident Review**  
   Every significant incident shall undergo a lessons-learned review. Corrective actions shall be implemented and tracked (A.5.27).

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **All Personnel:** Report incidents immediately.
- **Incident Response Team:** Coordinate response, containment, and recovery.
- **Management:** Provide resources and approve major decisions.
- **Legal/Compliance:** Advise on regulatory notifications and evidence.

## Compliance
Failure to report or follow this policy may result in disciplinary action (A.6.4).
