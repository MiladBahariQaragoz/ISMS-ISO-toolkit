# Information Security Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer (fictional)  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership, Aether Labs  
**Review Date:** 2027-07-11  
**Classification:** Internal

## Purpose
This policy establishes the framework for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets at Aether Labs. It defines the principles and responsibilities for information security to support business objectives while complying with applicable laws and standards, including ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who have access to Aether Labs information assets, systems, and facilities. It covers all information in any form (electronic, paper, verbal) throughout its lifecycle.

## Definitions
- **Information Security:** The protection of information assets from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.
- **Information Assets:** Data, systems, hardware, software, and processes owned or controlled by Aether Labs.

## Policy Statements
1. **Security as a Business Priority**  
   Information security is integral to all business activities. Security requirements must be identified and addressed during the planning, development, and operation of all systems and processes (A.5.8, A.5.1).

2. **Risk-Based Approach**  
   Security controls shall be implemented based on risk assessments. Risks shall be identified, assessed, treated, and monitored in accordance with the Risk Management Policy (A.5.9, A.5.15).

3. **Legal and Regulatory Compliance**  
   Aether Labs shall comply with all applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements regarding information security (A.5.31, A.5.34).

4. **Continuous Improvement**  
   The information security management system (ISMS) shall be reviewed and improved regularly through audits, incident learning, and management reviews (A.5.35, A.5.27).

5. **Awareness and Training**  
   All personnel shall receive appropriate information security awareness, education, and training (A.6.3).

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Executive Leadership:** Provide resources, approve policies, and demonstrate commitment to information security (A.5.4).
- **CISO / Security Team:** Develop, implement, and maintain the ISMS; advise on security matters.
- **All Employees:** Comply with this policy, report incidents, and complete required training (A.6.8).
- **Third Parties:** Adhere to security requirements as defined in supplier agreements (A.5.19, A.5.20).

## Compliance
Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, in accordance with the Disciplinary Process (A.6.4).

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the CISO and must be documented with compensating controls.
