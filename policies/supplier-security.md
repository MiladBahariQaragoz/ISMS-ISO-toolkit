# Supplier and Third-Party Security Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-07-11  
**Owner:** Procurement / Security Team  
**Approved By:** Executive Leadership, Aether Labs  
**Review Date:** 2027-07-11  
**Classification:** Internal

## Purpose
This policy ensures that information security risks arising from suppliers and third parties are identified, assessed, and managed. It establishes requirements for secure supplier relationships (A.5.19, A.5.20).

## Scope
This policy applies to all suppliers, vendors, contractors, and service providers that process, store, or have access to Aether Labs information assets or systems.

## Definitions
- **Supplier:** Any external party providing goods or services to Aether Labs.
- **Due Diligence:** Process of evaluating a supplier's security posture before engagement.

## Policy Statements
1. **Supplier Selection and Onboarding**  
   Security requirements shall be included in supplier selection criteria. Due diligence assessments (questionnaires, audits, certifications) shall be performed based on risk level (A.5.19).

2. **Contractual Requirements**  
   Contracts with suppliers must include information security clauses covering data protection, incident notification, right to audit, and compliance with Aether Labs policies (A.5.20).

3. **Ongoing Monitoring**  
   Supplier performance and security posture shall be monitored throughout the relationship. Changes in risk shall trigger reassessment (A.5.22).

4. **Access by Suppliers**  
   Supplier access to Aether Labs systems and data must be limited, logged, and revoked when no longer required. Suppliers must comply with Aether Labs access policies (A.5.15).

5. **Incident and Breach Notification**  
   Suppliers must notify Aether Labs of security incidents affecting our data or services within defined timeframes (e.g., 24 hours) (A.5.26).

6. **Termination and Exit**  
   Upon contract termination, all Aether Labs data and access must be returned or securely destroyed, and access revoked (A.5.11).

7. **Supply Chain Risk**  
   Critical suppliers shall be required to manage risks in their own supply chains (A.5.21).

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Procurement:** Include security in RFPs and contracts.
- **Security Team:** Conduct assessments and monitor compliance.
- **Business Owners:** Define requirements for suppliers supporting their processes.
- **Suppliers:** Meet contractual security obligations.

## Compliance
Non-compliance by suppliers may result in contract termination. Internal violations will be handled per the Disciplinary Process (A.6.4).
